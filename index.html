Why we love global cuisine

Food is the easiest way to travel without leaving home. Every dish carries a story: the climate it grew up in, the trade routes that brought its spices, and the families who passed it down. A bowl of Egyptian Koshari, a plate of grilled steak from Texas-raised beef, or a bright shrimp salad from the Mediterranean coast each tells you something about the people who first made it.

On this site we share the recipes we love, fun facts about where they come from, and a gallery of dishes. Grab a fork, spoon, or chopsticks and dig in!
