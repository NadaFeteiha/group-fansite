Tunisia and/or region of North Africa

Shakshuka

Fun fact: Nobody agrees on what the name means.

  • The name comes from Maghrebi Arabic and means “mixed”.
  • The Oxford English Dictionary links the English word to an Arabic word that imitates a sound, related to a verb meaning to bubble, to sizzle, or to be mixed up.

Thailand

Tom Yum Soup

Fun fact: A soup gave its name to a financial crisis. The 1997 Asian financial crisis started in Thailand, and Thai people call it the “Tom Yum Kung crisis”.

  • The Bank of Thailand says the crisis began on 2 July 1997, the day Thailand announced it would float the baht and asked the IMF for help.
  • The central bank calls it the worst crisis in Thailand’s modern economic history.

Egypt

Koshari

Fun fact: A koshary seller is part chef, part street performer.

  • vendors toss each scoop through the air onto the plate and smack the ladle on the pot, which makes a clang.
  • Koshary is one of Egypt's cheapest meals, so people use it as an informal way to track inflation.

Mexico

Shrimp Ceviche

Fun fact: The shrimp turns pink without any heat. Lime juice “cooks” it.

  • The acid in lime juice breaks the weak bonds that hold proteins together. This is called denaturing, and it changes the color and texture of the meat, just like heat does.
  • The leftover juice has a funny name: "leche de tigre", or "tiger's milk". In Peru, people drink it as a shot and call it a hangover cure.
