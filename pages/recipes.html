Shakshuka

Eggs gently cooked in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Ready in 30 minutes and serves 2–3.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a pan and cook the onion and pepper until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, and paprika and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Make 4 small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover and cook until the egg whites are set, about 5–7 minutes.
  6. Top with parsley and serve with bread.

Koshari

Egypt's famous street food: rice, lentils, and pasta topped with a tangy tomato sauce and crispy onions. Takes about 1 hour and serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown lentils
  • 1 cup rice
  • 1 cup small pasta (elbow or ditalini)
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 can (400 g) tomato sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon cumin, salt, and chili flakes to taste

Steps

  1. Fry the sliced onions in oil until dark golden and crispy, then set aside.
  2. Boil the lentils until almost tender, then add the rice and cook together until done.
  3. Boil the pasta separately and drain.
  4. For the sauce, cook the garlic in a little oil, add the tomato sauce, vinegar, cumin, and salt, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer rice and lentils, pasta, and chickpeas in a bowl.
  6. Pour the sauce on top and finish with the crispy onions.

Tom Yum Soup

A hot and sour broth with lemongrass, lime leaves, chili, and shrimp that wakes up every taste bud. Ready in 30 minutes and serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 1 liter chicken or shrimp stock
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, cut into pieces and smashed
  • 4 kaffir lime leaves, torn
  • 3 slices galangal or ginger
  • 2–3 Thai chilies, smashed
  • 200 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 300 g shrimp, peeled
  • 3 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 3 tablespoons lime juice
  • Fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Bring the stock to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Add the lemongrass, lime leaves, galangal, and chilies and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the mushrooms and cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the shrimp and cook until they turn pink, about 2–3 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat and stir in the fish sauce and lime juice.
  6. Taste, adjust the sour and salty balance, and top with cilantro.

Steak with Chimichurri

Flame-grilled beef topped with a punchy sauce of parsley, garlic, oregano, and red wine vinegar. Ready in 30 minutes and serves 2.

Ingredients

  • 2 steaks (ribeye or flank), about 250 g each
  • 1 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Mix the parsley, garlic, oregano, pepper flakes, vinegar, and olive oil in a bowl. Season with salt and let it rest.
  2. Take the steaks out of the fridge 20 minutes before cooking and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a grill or heavy pan until very hot.
  4. Cook the steaks for 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  5. Let the steaks rest for 5 minutes, then slice them.
  6. Spoon the chimichurri over the top and serve.

Shrimp Ceviche

Fresh shrimp "cooked" in lime juice with tomato, onion, cilantro, and avocado. Perfect on a hot day. Ready in 45 minutes and serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 500 g raw shrimp, peeled and chopped
  • 1 cup fresh lime juice (about 8 limes)
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 jalapeño, finely diced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • Handful of fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Salt and tortilla chips for serving

Steps

  1. Put the shrimp in a glass bowl and cover it with the lime juice.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for 20–30 minutes, until the shrimp turns pink and firm.
  3. Drain off most of the lime juice.
  4. Stir in the tomatoes, onion, jalapeño, and cilantro, and season with salt.
  5. Gently fold in the avocado just before serving.
  6. Serve cold with tortilla chips.
